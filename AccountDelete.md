# Delete Your Account — Local Allergies

You can request deletion of your Local Allergies account and all associated data at any time. Your request will be processed within **30 days**.

---

## Option 1 — Delete directly in the app

The fastest way to delete your account is from within Local Allergies:

1. Open **Local Allergies**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Data & Privacy**
4. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm

This immediately removes your account and queues deletion of all server-side data.

## What data is deleted

| Data | Outcome | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Account (name & email address) | Deleted | Within 30 days |
| Saved locations & preferences | Deleted | Within 30 days |
| Subscription entitlements (RevenueCat) | Deleted | Within 30 days |
| Symptom logs & environmental snapshots | Deleted (on-device) | Immediately on uninstall, or via Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Logs |
| Purchase / subscription records | Retained | Up to 7 years as required by financial regulations |

> **Note on on-device data:** Symptom logs, environmental snapshots, allergen model outputs, and app preferences are stored only on your device — they are never uploaded to our servers. We cannot delete these remotely. To remove them, use **Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete All Logs** in the app before uninstalling.
